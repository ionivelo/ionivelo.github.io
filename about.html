About

Making every Indian home smart

ioNivelo started with a simple observation: most Indian homes have modular switchboards, but no way to control them from a phone. The existing solutions require rewiring, expensive hubs, or new switchboards entirely.

The problem

Smart home products in India assume you're building a new home or willing to tear open your walls. For the 200+ million homes that already have working switchboards, there was no good option.

Our approach

ioNivelo fits behind your existing switchboard. No rewiring, no new faceplates, nothing chased into walls. A trained installer fits it in about an hour and hands it back working. Your wall switches keep working exactly as they do today.

What we believe

  • •Smart home shouldn't require rebuilding your home.
  • •Local control matters more than cloud features.
  • •Honesty beats marketing. We don't claim features we haven't validated.
  • •The device should work even when the internet doesn't.
